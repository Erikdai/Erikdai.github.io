Hi! I am Chengxiao Dai, a Master of Computer Science student and tutor at the University of Sydney. I have previously worked as a research intern at A*STAR’s Singapore Institute of Manufacturing Technology and the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT), and as an algorithm engineer at Imperial Vision. I am also a co-founder of ChuChiang Data, where we focus on building high-quality synthetic data solutions to address long-tail data challenges.

My research focuses on large language models and multi-agent systems, in particular LLM post-training, reinforcement learning for agentic systems, knowledge-graph reasoning, and domain-specific applications.
